# PDF to Text Converter

Web-based PDF to text converter with Gujarati OCR support.

## Usage

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Upload or drag-drop your PDF file
3. Click "Convert to Text"
4. Download as TXT or Word format

## Features

- ✅ Web-based UI (no installation needed)
- ✅ Drag & drop PDF upload
- ✅ Gujarati + Hindi + English OCR
- ✅ Progress tracking
- ✅ Download as TXT or Word
- ✅ Processes up to 10 pages
- ✅ Works offline after first load
